How should I interpret these results?
  • The NLP model reads your written description to predict an overall skin type.
  • The CNN classifies the uploaded image into a skin type.
  • The intensity model rates oiliness, dryness and sensitivity as Low, Medium or High.
  • These are model predictions, not medical diagnoses.
  • The models work independently and may disagree.
