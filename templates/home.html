Oily skin

What it means: Your skin produces more sebum (natural oil) than average.

Common traits: A shiny look, visible pores, and a tendency to feel greasy.

Through the day: Skin often looks fine in the morning and shinier by midday.

How it differs: Unlike dry skin, it rarely feels tight. Unlike combination skin, the oiliness is not limited to the T-zone.

Dry skin

What it means: Your skin holds less moisture than average.

Common traits: A tight feeling, rough patches, dullness, or flaking.

Through the day: Skin may feel tight after washing and more uncomfortable by evening.

How it differs: Unlike oily skin, it rarely looks shiny. Unlike normal skin, it often needs extra attention to comfort.

Normal skin

What it means: Your skin is generally well balanced.

Common traits: Even texture, few blemishes, and little shine or flaking.

Through the day: Skin feels comfortable from morning to night.

How it differs: It sits between oily and dry, without the strong tendencies of either.

Combination skin

What it means: Different areas of your face behave like different skin types.

Common traits: An oily T-zone (forehead, nose, chin) with normal or drier cheeks.

Through the day: The center of the face may get shiny while the cheeks stay comfortable or feel tight.

How it differs: It mixes traits of oily and dry skin instead of showing one throughout.

How does DERMA / INSIGHT work?

We use your skin description and/or image to estimate your skin type.

  • The NLP model analyzes the skin description.
  • The CNN model analyzes the uploaded image.
  • The available prediction information is processed by the existing application.
  • The result is displayed on the existing results page.

DERMA / INSIGHT predicts skin type only. It does not diagnose skin conditions or recommend medical treatment.
